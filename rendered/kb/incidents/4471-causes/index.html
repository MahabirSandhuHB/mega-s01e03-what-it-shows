By 4 October, ticket #4471 held 412 unique reports about dates showing the wrong day. Deduplicating the export by report ID and checking each tenant's locale and booking form shows two different problems, not one.

What the reports show

The shared parser treats dates as month-first. In day-first tenants, dates entered outside the booking-v3 form can therefore flip the day and month. These 379 reports arrived from 1 April through 4 October 2026. Intake tagged 356 as parser and 23 as hint, which shows why the intake tag alone cannot identify the cause.

The other 33 reports came from booking-v3 forms used by day-first tenants. Those forms instruct people to enter MM/DD/YYYY, while the tenant reads the date day-first. The reports came from alder.physio (12), copper.studio (11), and pale.harbour (10), from 17 April through 22 September 2026. Intake tagged 18 as hint and 15 as parser.

The export contains 427 rows because 15 report IDs appear more than once. Each report ID is counted once above. The cause split uses the tenant locale and form behavior described in src/lib/kb/causes.ts, not the intake tag.
